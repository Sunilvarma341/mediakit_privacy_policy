Privacy Policy

App Name: MyMediaKit

Last Updated: April 6, 2026

1. Introduction

MyMediaKit is committed to protecting your privacy. This app works completely offline and does not collect or share any personal data.

2. What We Do

  • Compress images
  • Compress videos
  • Convert images to PDF
  • Save files to your device

3. Data Collection

We DO NOT collect:

  • Personal data
  • Location
  • Device information
  • Usage analytics

4. Permissions

Permission Purpose
Camera Capture images for PDF
Storage / Media Select and save files

5. Data Usage

All processing happens locally on your device. Files are never uploaded or shared with any server.

6. Third-Party Services

We do not use analytics, ads, or any third-party tracking services.

7. User Control

  • You control all your files
  • You can delete files anytime
  • You can revoke permissions anytime

Your privacy is our priority.
